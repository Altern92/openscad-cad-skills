# Wave 3 Acceptance Criteria (2026-11-28) — bounded tier

Source: NEXT.md P39, P40, P41. Oracles: Bethune, *Engineering Design and Graphics with SolidWorks 2016*.

- **AC-W3-1 (P40)** Given a fastener with `position_tol` T, when its clearance hole is narrower than F + T
  (through-bolt, H − T = F, §8-43) or F + 2T (`into`/`insert`, H − 2T = F, §8-46), then check_fasteners FAILs
  (exit 3) naming the formula. Oracle §8-48: Ø20 holes, T 0.03 → floating 19.97, fixed 19.94; fixture tests
  19.96/19.99 floating and 19.93/19.96 fixed. Negative `position_tol` = exit 2.
  Check: `tests/fixtures/fasteners_position_tol`; mutants `fasteners_tol_ignored`, `fasteners_tol_fixed_half`,
  `fasteners_tol_negative_ok`.
- **AC-W3-2 (P41)** Given an `into` screw in a blind hole, when the hole ends less than 2 pitches past the tip,
  then a WARN "< 2P" is printed and exit stays 0; a through hole or ≥ 2P gives no WARN. Oracle §6-13:
  M10×1.25×25 through 10 mm cover → hole 18 deep OK, 17 deep WARN (2.0 < 2.5).
  Check: `tests/fixtures/fasteners_2p`; mutants `fasteners_no_2p`, `fasteners_2p_one_pitch`.
- **AC-W3-3 (P39)** Given any fixture that makes a checker FAIL, when its output is audited, then every FAIL
  block carries "Hint:" (what to change). Check: `tests/fixtures/fail_hints` + one strip-a-hint mutant.

Not in scope: bolt protrusion past a nut (§6-11) — fasteners.json has no nut position; add when a nut is declared.
