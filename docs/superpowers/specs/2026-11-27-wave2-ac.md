## E. P31 — more strength formulas (`check_strength.py`, `loads.json`, `references/`)

- **AC-E1 thread strip-out.** Given a `loads.json` case of type `screw_pullout` (screw into a
  printed hole or heat-set insert: thread size, engagement length, axial force, material), then the
  check computes the thread shear area (ISO/Machinery's Handbook formula for internal thread,
  cited) and FAILs when shear stress × safety > material shear strength (from `materials.json`;
  if the material has no shear value, use 0.6 × tensile with a ◇ note → WARN only).
- **AC-E2 press-fit hoop stress.** Given a `press_fit` case (shaft Ø, hub outer Ø, interference,
  hub material E and strength), then the Lamé thick-wall formula (cited) gives contact pressure and
  hub hoop stress; FAIL when hoop stress × safety > tensile strength along the layers that carry it.
- **AC-E3 bracket bending/shear.** Existing beam check extended with transverse shear
  (τ = 1.5 V/A for a rectangular section, cited) at every station; FAIL when shear governs.
- **AC-E4.** Each formula has an analytic reference case in its fixture (hand-computed number in
  a comment, checker output equal within 1 %), a failing and a passing case, and a mutant.

## F. P32 `mate()` + P36 untoleranced contacts + ISO 286 fits (new `check_fits.py`, gate `fits`)

- **AC-F1 `mate()` pattern.** `openscad-cad/references/patterns.scad` gains a `mate()` pattern:
  one module that both parts `include`, with `removing=true` producing the cutter (peg Ø +
  clearance, optional head relief) and `removing=false` the peg/shaft; clearance read from one
  named variable in `params.scad`. Example usage in a comment; renders in a fixture.
- **AC-F2 hole = peg + clearance.** `joints.json` contact may declare `"fit": {"peg": part,
  "hole": part, "clearance_mm": c}` (or `"iso286": "H7/g6"`, `"nominal_mm": d`). The check
  measures the peg Ø and the hole Ø at the contact (ray/section) and FAILs when hole − peg is
  outside [c − tol, c + tol] (tol from the print tolerance table, provisional 0.05 mm) or, for
  ISO 286, outside the class limits (table subset H7, H8, g6, h6, k6, p6 for 3–50 mm, cited).
  Mutant: hole 0.3 mm too small → FAIL.
- **AC-F3 untoleranced contacts (P36).** Two parts whose surfaces are within 0.3 mm (◇) of each
  other over an area ≥ 1 mm² (◇) but are not declared in `joints.json` (contact / fit / expected
  interference) → WARN by default, FAIL with `--strict` (provisional: WARN because the numbers
  are ◇). Lists the pair and the contact location.
- **AC-F4.** Wired into `validate_scad.sh --all` as `CHECK_RESULT fits=…` (SKIP when no fit is
  declared and no near-contact found), fixture, mutant, `validation.md` section.

## G. P32 gears — worm and bevel (`motion_sweep.py` `check_gear_axes`)

- **AC-G1.** A `gear_mesh` contact with `"kind": "bevel"` and `module`: the axes must intersect
  (distance between axis lines ≤ 0.05 mm) and the angle between them must equal the declared
  shaft angle (default 90°) within 0.1°; FAIL otherwise. Pitch-cone apex check: both apexes at the
  axes' intersection within 0.1 mm (provisional, if BOSL2 exposes the apex).
- **AC-G2.** `"kind": "worm"` with `module`, worm pitch radius and gear teeth: axes must be
  perpendicular (90° ± 0.1°) and their common-normal distance must equal BOSL2's worm/worm-gear
  centre distance (`worm_dist()` if available, else pitch radii sum, cited) within 0.05 mm.
- **AC-G3.** Undeclared `kind` on non-parallel axes keeps today's NOT CHECKED line. The RC
  `steering_reduction_gearbox` case (worm/bevel declared as plain `gear_mesh`) is documented in
  the fixture comment; the RC project itself is NOT edited.

## H. P32 screw engagement + P20b (`check_fasteners.py`)

- **AC-H1 thread engagement.** For each declared screw into a printed/insert hole, engaged
  length = overlap of the screw's threaded length with material along its axis; FAIL when
  < 1.5 × d for plastic (◇ unless a cited source is found; then cite) or < insert length for
  heat-set inserts; FAIL when the screw tip hits material before the head seats (bottoming out).
- **AC-H2 P20b.** The host-hole inventory checks every contact plane of a mounted part (all
  planar faces within 0.1 mm of the host), not only the largest; fixture: a part touching the
  host on two planes covering a host hole on the smaller one → FAIL.

## I. P38 rest (`check_printability.py`)

- **AC-I1** knife edges: convex edges with dihedral < 45° (◇ text-to-cad's own, Hubs 45° as
  context) reported as WARN with location. **AC-I2** stair stepping: report shallow up-facing
  areas (< 20°, ◇) as INFO with layer height `--layer` (default 0.2, ◇). **AC-I3** support
  volume estimate: INFO (mm³ under flagged overhang regions down to the plate/part below).
  None of these change the exit code (all ◇). Fixture + semantic mutant each.
